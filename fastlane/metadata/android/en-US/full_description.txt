The complete book "Mukhtasar al-Nasiha fi al-Adhkar wa al-Ad'iya al-Sahiha", authored by Muhammad Ahmad Ismail al-Muqaddam, in the Zad Al-Dhakir app. The app's text was compared page by page with the revised tenth edition (1446 AH / 2025).

For anyone looking for daily adhkar or a dua from the Quran and Sunnah, the app brings together the book's chapters on daily practice, with their text and sources as printed in the book.

Contents:
• Morning and evening adhkar, sleep and waking, prayer, adhan and the mosque, food, travel, Hajj and Umrah, illness and death, and the book's other chapters.
• Unrestricted adhkar and their virtues: the Quran, sending blessings on the Prophet ﷺ, tahlil, istighfar, al-baqiyat al-salihat and hawqala.
• Unrestricted supplications, the times they are answered, and the eleven wirds.
• The chapters' introductions, explanations, notes and footnotes, where the book places them.

Features:
• A hierarchical index in the book's order that expands in place.
• Search across the index and the text, showing where each result sits.
• A counter for counted adhkar: tap the dhikr itself to count, or use the volume keys.
• Favourites and saved reading and counting progress.
• Daily reminders for morning, evening and sleep adhkar, with morning and evening times based on prayer times in your city.
• Share a dhikr as text or as an image, or copy it.
• Choice of text size and typeface, and dark mode.
• Works fully offline, with no ads and no data collection.
